About Algeria Pulse

Algeria Pulse is a lightweight, serverless way to turn a local idea, event, or breaking story into a link you can send anywhere — no account, no backend, no data storage.

1. Pick a wilaya

Choose your province from the dropdown or tap it directly on the interactive map.

2. Write your pulse

Type your idea, event, or news update in the text field, in Arabic or English.

3. Get your link

A unique link is generated instantly, encoding the timestamp, wilaya, and your message.

4. Share it anywhere

Copy or share the link through any app — Algeria Pulse never stores your content.

100% client-side, zero server storage Full Arabic / English support Light & dark Green Forest theme Interactive 58-wilaya map
