# Algeria Pulse — نبض الجزائر

![version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-2.4.0-2E6F40)

Share a local idea, event, or piece of breaking news from any of Algeria's
**58 wilayas** — as a single link you can send anywhere. No account, no
sign-up, nothing stored on a server.

---

## What is Algeria Pulse?

Algeria Pulse turns a short message tied to a specific wilaya into a link.
Pick your province, write what's happening, and get a link back instantly —
ready to paste into a chat, a post, or a message. Anyone who opens that link
sees exactly what you wrote and where it's from.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Interactive map of Algeria** — right below the wilaya dropdown, tap
  any of the 58 wilayas for a smooth, fluid zoom-in and a radar-style pulse
  animation, with crisp, clearly visible boundaries between every wilaya.
  The selected wilaya's code and name are shown as two neat little chips
  underneath.
- 🌍 **Arabic & English** — switch languages with one tap; the whole app,
  including text direction, flips instantly.
- 🌓 **Light & dark mode** — an easy-on-the-eyes Green Forest look, day or night.
- 📰 **A ticker that's actually yours** — a scrolling strip under the clock
  showing your own recent local activity: pulses you've created or opened,
  pulled straight from your local feed.
- 🕒 **Live clock** — always know exactly what time it is where you are.
- ✍️ **Simple composer** — write your idea, event, or news update in a clean
  text box, in either language.
- 🔎 **Expand to write comfortably** — tap the expand icon above the text
  box to pop it out into a larger, focused writing space, complete with
  live character, word, and line counts. Collapse it and your text (and
  cursor) is exactly where you left it.
- 🏷️ **Tags — automatic, presets, or your own** — every pulse carries a
  default tag automatically (**#Pulse** in English, **#نبضة** in Arabic).
  On top of that, flag it as Urgent, an Alert, about Services, or Traffic
  with one tap, or type any hashtag of your own into the tag field.
- 🔗 **Instant shareable links, only when it's ready** — a link is only
  generated once you've picked a wilaya, written something, and have at
  least one tag active (the default tag already covers that last one) — no
  incomplete or empty links ever get created.
- 📋 **Two ways to copy** — copy the plain link, or copy a ready-to-post
  **formatted snippet** (wilaya, your message, who posted it, and the link
  all in one block) for pasting straight into social media.
- 🕓 **Live "time ago" stamps** — once your link exists, see at a glance how
  long ago it was posted — just now, an hour ago, last week — updating in
  real time. (It only shows once there's actually a link to attach it to.)
- 🙋 **A little avatar for every poster** — a small circular badge next to
  the username shows the first letter of their name, or a friendly
  incognito icon if they posted anonymously. Tap your own avatar or your
  name at any time to change it — no separate edit button needed.
- 💾 **Remembers your name** — once you've entered a name, Algeria Pulse
  remembers it on your device for next time, so you don't have to retype it
  every visit. You can still change it, or go anonymous, whenever you like.
- 🔔 **Toast confirmations & crisp sound cues** — a friendly little
  notification and a distinct chime confirm every copy, share, and save —
  with a lower, different tone if you try to interact with something
  that's locked, and yet another for map and dropdown selections.
- 🕘 **Your local feed** — every pulse you create or open is quietly saved
  on your own device (never anywhere else). Open it from the clock-history
  icon in the header to search, filter (All / Mine / Viewed), reopen, or
  delete entries — and it's all just as easy to clear.
- 🙂 **Enter a name, or stay anonymous** — the choice is yours every time you
  post; nothing is ever recorded if you choose to stay anonymous.
- 🔒 **Reading is read-only** — opened a link someone shared with you? The
  map and dropdown lock while you're viewing it, so you can't accidentally
  overwrite their pulse — trying anyway plays a distinct "locked" sound.
- ✏️ **A floating "New idea" button** — always on screen, one tap clears
  the box, unlocks everything, scrolls you straight to the text field, and
  asks who's posting (or lets you stay anonymous).
- ⚠️ **A quick reminder before you post** — a short, collapsible notice about
  posting responsibly and staying within Algeria's publishing regulations.
- ⬆️⬇️ **Floating scroll buttons** — step through the page's key sections
  one tap at a time, from the top all the way to the footer and back.
- 📱 **Works everywhere** — phone, tablet, or desktop.

## 🚀 How to use it

1. **Pick your wilaya** — choose it from the dropdown, or tap it directly on
   the map right underneath.
2. **Write your pulse** — your idea, a local event, or a bit of news, in
   Arabic or English. Need more room? Tap the expand icon.
3. **Tag it, if you'd like** — the default tag is already added for you;
   layer on a preset (Urgent, Alert, Services, Traffic) or type your own
   into the tag field and hit Enter.
4. **Say who you are (or don't)** — the first time you post, you'll be
   asked for a name (already filled in if you've used Algeria Pulse on this
   device before). You can also just continue as **Anonymous** — nothing
   about you is recorded either way.
5. **Get your link** — as soon as your wilaya, message, and tag are all in
   place, it's generated instantly.
6. **Share it** — copy the plain link, copy a formatted snippet ready for
   social media, or tap the share icon to send it straight through any app.

### Opened a link someone sent you?

You'll see their wilaya, their message, any tags they added, who shared it
(or "Anonymous"), and roughly when — "5 minutes ago", "yesterday", and so
on. The map, dropdown, and text box are all locked while you're reading, so
there's no risk of changing someone else's pulse by accident. Tap the
floating **New idea** button whenever you're ready to clear it and write
your own — that's the moment you'll be asked for your name (or to stay
anonymous) for your own post.

### Changing your name later

Changed your mind about your name, or want to go anonymous instead? Just
tap your avatar or your username, right in the text section — no hunting
for a separate edit button.

### Your local feed

Every pulse you write or open is quietly saved on your own device, never
anywhere else. Tap the history icon in the header to search it, filter to
just your own posts or ones you've viewed, reopen any entry, or clear it
all. The news ticker under the clock is powered by this same feed, so your
most recent activity is always right there at the top of the page too.

---

## 🔒 A note on privacy

Algeria Pulse doesn't have a server, a database, or accounts. Every link
contains everything it needs to display your pulse — nothing is uploaded or
stored anywhere else. Your local feed and your remembered name live only in
your own browser, on your own device — Algeria Pulse itself never sees
them. If you choose to stay anonymous, no name is ever attached to your
link at all.

## 🧰 Built with

Algeria Pulse is a small, dependency-light app that leans on a couple of
focused open-source libraries, loaded straight from a CDN — no build step,
no server:

- **[D3.js](https://d3js.org/)** powers the interactive map: locating each
  wilaya's center, and driving the smooth zoom/pan and radar-pulse
  animations.
- **[Howler.js](https://howlerjs.com/)** handles every sound effect —
  selection beeps, the "locked" tone, and copy/save chimes — reliably
  across browsers and devices.

The app itself is organized as native ES6 modules, one file per concern:

```
index.html
css/
  variables.css   → design tokens (colors, spacing, type)
  main.css        → components, layout, responsive rules
js/
  config.js       → bilingual (EN/AR) text and hashtag catalog
  audio.js        → Howler-powered sound engine
  map.js          → D3-powered map (zoom/pan, sensor-pulse rings)
  storage.js      → localStorage: feed archive + remembered username
  ui.js           → all DOM rendering — avatars, modals, toasts, ticker
  app.js          → app state, event wiring, link-generation rules
```

Everything loads directly in the browser — no build step, no bundler,
no package manager. Just open `index.html` through a local server (browsers
block ES module imports over the bare `file://` protocol).
